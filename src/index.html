Thicknesses are estimated from typical page counts and edition trim sizes (roughly 460 pages per inch of paper, plus cover allowance for hardcovers) — a specific printing on your own shelf may run thinner or thicker. "Story set" is the period a book's narrative covers: for novels and memoirs that's the events on the page; for nonfiction like a history or a biography, it's the era the book focuses on. A few titles — invented worlds, or nonfiction spanning centuries — don't have a single year and are grouped separately when arranged by setting.
